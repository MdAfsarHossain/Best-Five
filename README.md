# [Manchester City](https://mdafsarhossain.github.io/Best-Five/)

Introducing My New `Manchester City` Website! Dive into all things Manchester City with my new interactive website. It showcases the `latest stats` on player `goals` and `assists`, allowing you to handpick your `top 5 players`, assign `custom prices`, and even include a `coach` and `manager` in your team. With `real-time` cost calculations, you can build your dream squad in seconds! The site also keeps things in check, giving you warnings if you exceed five players or enter invalid prices.

## Key Features:

- Display of all Manchester City players’ goals and assists.
- Select up to 5 favorite players and customize their price.
- Include a coach and manager for a complete team setup.
- Instant cost calculation for your selected squad.
- Smart alerts for exceeding player limits or entering invalid price values.

## Technologies

- Javascript
- DOM
- HTML
- CSS
- Tailwind
- DaisyUI
